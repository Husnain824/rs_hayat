The Beginning

Early Life & Background

Family & Roots

Born on 18 February 1987 in Phool Nagar, Kasur District — a town closely tied to his family's roots and public legacy.

He belongs to a prominent political family. His father, Rana Muhammad Hayat Khan, serves as MNA from NA-134 Kasur-IV, while his uncle, Rana Muhammad Iqbal Khan, has served as Speaker of the Punjab Assembly and Governor of Punjab.

Much of his youth was spent abroad for study and exposure before returning home to serve Kasur and Punjab.
